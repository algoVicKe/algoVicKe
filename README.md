### The Idea

</div>

<div align="center">

Most AI work stops at the model.<br>
Real systems don't. They have to be designed, secured, shipped, observed, and maintained.<br><br>
Everything here is built around that idea: <b>intelligence, security, infrastructure, and engineering as one discipline.</b>
